Privacy Policy

Instagram tools of @motion.in.code · Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short: if you comment a keyword under one of my reels, an automated tool sends you a direct message with what you asked for. To do that it processes your Instagram username, your Instagram user ID, your comment, the messages you send to the account and whether you follow it. Nothing is sold, used for advertising or shared beyond what is described below. Records are deleted after 90 days at the latest, or sooner on request (how to delete your data).

1. Who is responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Ehsan Tatasadi
Hamburg, Germany
Email: ehsan@tatasadi.com

2. What this policy covers

I use a Meta app (Instagram API with Instagram Login) to run my own Instagram accounts. It does two things:

  • Publishing my own reels and stories. This uses no data about other people.
  • Automatic replies on @motion.in.code: when you comment a keyword that a reel mentions (for example “PROMPT”), you get a private message with a button. When you tap it, you receive the promised content if you follow the account, or a request to follow first.

3. Which data is processed

Only what Instagram sends to the app when you interact with the account:

  • when you comment: your Instagram username, your Instagram user ID, the comment’s ID and text, the ID of the reel, and the time;
  • when you tap the button or write to the account: your message or button tap, and whether you follow the account (Instagram only shows this after you have messaged or tapped);
  • a record of what was sent to you and when, so that you do not get the same message twice.

The app does not receive your password, email address, phone number or contacts, and does not read your other messages or comments.

4. Why, and on which legal basis

  • To send you the content you asked for by commenting the keyword and tapping the button (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To check whether you follow the account, to avoid duplicate messages and to keep the tool running reliably (technical logs). This is my legitimate interest in running the automation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

There is no profiling and no advertising. The follow check only decides whether the content is sent now or after you follow.

5. Where the data is processed and who receives it

  • Microsoft Azure (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd. / Microsoft Corporation) hosts the tool in the Germany West Central region (Frankfurt) and processes the data on my behalf under Microsoft’s Data Protection Addendum. Microsoft is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Microsoft privacy statement.
  • Instagram is operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. Your comments and messages are processed by Meta under its own policy: Instagram privacy policy.

Nobody else receives the data.

6. How long the data is kept

  • The record of what was sent to you (username, user ID, keyword, time): deleted 90 days after your last interaction at the latest.
  • Technical logs: deleted automatically after 90 days at the latest.
  • The messages themselves stay in the Instagram conversation, like any direct message. You can delete the conversation on your side in Instagram at any time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). Write to the email address above.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for me: Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

8. How to delete your data

To have all data about you deleted, do one of the following:

  • send a direct message with the words DELETE MY DATA to @motion.in.code, or
  • send an email with your Instagram username to ehsan@tatasadi.com.

I will delete every record about you within 30 days and confirm it. If you do nothing, the data is deleted automatically as described in section 6.

9. Changes

If the tool changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
